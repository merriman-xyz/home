Selected work

Ingrid

A local-first grid of grids for structured thinking. Any tile can hold a grid of its own: text, to-dos, images, audio and code side by side. It works offline with no account, with optional multi-device sync.

I built the original editor, then extended it through AI-assisted development with multi-device sync and subscription billing.

Anyi Data

A local analytics workbench for large data. Pivot, filter and sort millions of rows in the browser, from your own CSV, JSON, Arrow or Parquet files or sixteen public datasets. Your rows stay on your computer.

I direct its development and make the product and design decisions, using AI coding agents to build a virtualized JavaScript grid over DuckDB-WASM.

Showdown Elo

MLB player ratings from regular-season plate appearances. Rebuilt nightly during the season, with career and season ratings since 2015, player search, rankings and recent movers.

I built the automated ratings site and its nightly publication pipeline.

Yunshang Tea Design
